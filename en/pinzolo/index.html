Pinzolo · Val Rendena

30 metres from the gondola

We're a 5-minute walk from the centre of Pinzolo. In winter, the lifts take you all the way to the pistes of Madonna di Campiglio. In summer, you can go hiking or cycling.

Hotel Maffei › Pinzolo and the gondola
Winter

From our front door to the pistes

The Pinzolo–Prà Rodont gondola starts 30 metres from the hotel: its bottom station is on our street. From Ski Area Pinzolo, the Pinzolo–Campiglio Express takes you to the pistes of Madonna di Campiglio in a lift ride of about 16 minutes. It is all part of the Skiarea Campiglio Dolomiti di Brenta: over 150 km of pistes on a single ski pass.

Breakfast is served from 7:30. Then you head out with your skis, and a few steps later you're at the gondola. In the evening, your skis and boots stay in our ski room.

  • Gondola30 metres away, on our street
  • PistesOver 150 km, one ski pass
  • Ski and boot roomAt the hotel

Summer

Hiking and cycling in Val Rendena

All around us are the Brenta Dolomites and the Adamello Brenta Nature Park. You can set off on a hike or explore the valley by bike.

When you get back, our garden and terrace are waiting for you. If you'd like the Trentino Guest Card, just ask us and we'll send it to you before you arrive.

Getting to Pinzolo

You'll find us at Via N. Bolognini 72, 38086 Pinzolo (TN). For transport details, please see the official Madonna di Campiglio Dolomiti pages. If you have any questions about your journey, write to us and we'll reply personally.
