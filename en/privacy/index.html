Privacy and cookies

On this page we explain what personal data we handle when you contact us or book, and which cookies our website uses. Last updated: 1 November 2026.

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is our business, L&M snc di Scaramuzzi Luca e Pederzolli Manuela, which runs Hotel Garnì Maffei. Address: Via N. Bolognini 72, 38086 Pinzolo (TN). VAT number 02588730222.

If you have any questions about your data, email us at info@hotelmaffeipinzolo.it. We, Luca and Manuela, will reply to you personally.

What data we use and why

Enquiries and quotes. When you email us, message us on WhatsApp or phone us, we use your name, your contact details and what you ask us about, such as your dates and the number of people. We need this information to reply to you and to arrange your stay if you decide to book. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, the GDPR).

Bookings. When you book through our website, you're using the Beddy booking system (Zucchetti Hospitality). Beddy collects the details needed for your stay and for payment, in line with its own privacy notice, which you'll see while booking. We use these details to manage your stay (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Legal obligations. Like all accommodation providers in Italy, we send the details of everyone staying with us to the police, provide statistical data and meet our tax requirements (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR).

We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who we share your data with

We share your data only with the service providers we need to run the hotel (booking system, email provider, tax adviser) and, when the law requires it, with the authorities.

If you message us on WhatsApp, the service is provided by Meta under its own terms.

How long we keep your data

We keep your enquiries for as long as we need to reply to you and to manage your stay if you decide to book.

We keep booking details and tax records for as long as the law requires.

Our website and cookies

We don't use profiling cookies or analytics tools. The website itself remembers just one thing in your browser (in its local storage): that you've closed the cookie notice, so you won't see it again.

The booking bar is provided by Beddy (Zucchetti Hospitality). To make it work, the booking system and its payment provider, Stripe, use essential cookies, which are also needed to keep transactions secure.

The website is static and hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). For security reasons, GitHub records the IP address of anyone who visits our pages.

The map opens in Google Maps only if you click the link. We don't use Google Fonts: the fonts on our pages are hosted on our own website. The booking bar loads at your first action on the page (a tap, a click, the mouse, the keyboard or scrolling), or when a page opens already scrolled, for example from a link to one of the rooms. At that moment it downloads its files and its font from Beddy's and Stripe's servers, which therefore receive your device's IP address.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to give you access to your data, correct it, delete it, restrict how we use it, stop processing it or send you a copy (Articles 15–22 GDPR). Just email us at info@hotelmaffeipinzolo.it.

You also have the right to make a complaint to the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (www.garanteprivacy.it).
