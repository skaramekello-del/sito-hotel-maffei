Privacy policy

On this page we explain, as required by the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Articles 13 and 14), what personal data we handle when you contact us, book or stay with us. Cookies are covered on the Cookies page.

Who the controller is

The data controller is L&M snc di Scaramuzzi Luca e Pederzolli Manuela, the company that runs Hotel Garnì Maffei.

Address: Via N. Bolognini 72, 38086 Pinzolo (TN), Italy. VAT No. 02588730222, REA TN-235607. Email: info@hotelmaffeipinzolo.it. Certified email (PEC): lmsnc2020@pec.cgn.it.

If you have any questions about your data, write to us: Luca and Manuela will reply personally.

What data we handle

Contacts and enquiries. Your name, email, phone number and whatever you tell us by email, WhatsApp or phone: dates, number of guests, questions.

Bookings and stays. Dates, rooms, guests, price, payments and guarantees. If you book on our website, we collect them through the Beddy booking system; if you book through a portal such as Booking.com, the portal passes them on to us.

Guest registration. On arrival, Italian law requires us to send the details of each guest's identity document to the police (Article 109 of the Consolidated Law on Public Security), through the State Police's Alloggiati Web service.

Invoices, payments, tourist tax. The details needed for the invoice or receipt and for the provincial tourist tax.

Browsing data. Your IP address and the technical data that servers record when you visit the website: see the Cookies page for details.

We don't sell your data, we don't use it for advertising and we don't make automated decisions about you.

Who we share it with

We only share data with the parties we need in order to work, who handle it either as processors on our behalf (Art. 28 GDPR) or as independent controllers:

  • BookingsZucchetti Hospitality (Beddy booking system) and the providers it uses, such as Stripe, and the portals you book through, such as Booking.com.
  • PaymentsBanks and card networks: the card you give when booking is only a guarantee, and you pay for your stay at the hotel.
  • Email and messagesOur email providers, and Meta if you write to us on WhatsApp.
  • WebsiteGitHub, which hosts the website.
  • AdvisersOur tax adviser and, if needed, a lawyer.
  • AuthoritiesThe police, statistics offices, the Province for the tourist tax, the Revenue Agency and judicial authorities, when the law requires it.

We'll send you the up-to-date list of providers and their roles on request.

Data outside the European Union

Some providers are based in the United States, such as GitHub, Stripe and Meta. In these cases data is transferred on the basis of the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission (Art. 46 GDPR) and, where applicable, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision of July 2023). Otherwise, we only transfer data to countries that the Commission considers adequate (Art. 45 GDPR). You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards.

Your rights

You can exercise the rights set out in Articles 15–22 of the GDPR at any time:

  • Access (Art. 15)Find out what data we hold and get a copy.
  • Rectification (Art. 16)Correct or complete inaccurate data.
  • Erasure (Art. 17)Have it deleted, except data the law requires us to keep or that we need to defend a legal claim.
  • Restriction (Art. 18)Have its use suspended while a dispute is being checked.
  • Portability (Art. 20)Receive it in a machine-readable format, or have it sent to another controller.
  • Objection (Art. 21)Object to processing based on our legitimate interest.

Just write to info@hotelmaffeipinzolo.it or to our certified email address lmsnc2020@pec.cgn.it. We'll reply within one month; in complex cases this may be extended by two further months, and we'll tell you within the first month. It's free of charge, unless a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, Piazza Venezia 11, 00187 Rome – www.garanteprivacy.it) or with the supervisory authority of the EU country where you live or work.

How we protect your data

Only we and the providers we need can see your data, in systems protected by passwords. The website only works over https, so pages travel encrypted.

Updates

This policy may change, for example if our providers or the law change: the version in force is always on this page.
