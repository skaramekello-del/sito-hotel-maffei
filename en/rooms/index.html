Bed & breakfast

Our rooms

We have 13 rooms in five types: single, double, twin, triple and family, which sleeps up to four. They're bright and welcoming, with natural-toned floors and simple, practical furnishings. All of them have a private bathroom with shower, and breakfast is always included in the price.

Hotel Maffei › Rooms

Good to know before you book

  • Room and breakfastWe're a B&B (garni hotel): every morning from 7:30 to 9:30 a buffet breakfast awaits you, included in the price. For dinner, the restaurants in the centre are a few minutes' walk away. Restaurants we recommend
  • Winter staysIn high-season weeks we prefer week-long stays, usually arriving on Saturday. At quieter times you can also book fewer nights. You'll see the available dates and the minimum number of nights when you book online.
  • Arrival and departureCheck-in from 14:00, check-out by 10:00. Late check-out on request, subject to availability: until 14:00, €25; until 18:00, €50.
  • Tourist tax€2 per person per night, up to a maximum of 10 nights. Children under 14 are exempt. It isn't included in the price and is payable at the hotel, in cash.
  • PetsDogs are welcome on request, depending on room availability: please contact us before booking. We don't accept cats.
