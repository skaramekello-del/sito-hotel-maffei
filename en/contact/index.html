30 metres from the gondola

You'll find us at Via N. Bolognini 72, 38086 Pinzolo (TN), in Val Rendena. The bottom station of the Pinzolo–Prà Rodont gondola is on our street, 30 metres from the hotel. The centre of Pinzolo is a 5-minute walk. You can park for free wherever there's a space, in front of the hotel or a few metres away. A private garage is available on request, for an extra charge.

Frequently asked questions

Do you serve dinner or offer half board?

We're a B&B (garni hotel), offering room and breakfast only, with no half board. For dinner, the restaurants in the centre of Pinzolo are a few minutes' walk away. Restaurants we recommend

How do winter stays work?

In high-season weeks, stays run from Saturday to Saturday (7 nights) or from Saturday to Friday (6 nights). In quieter periods you can also book shorter stays: you'll see the available dates when you book online.

What are the times for breakfast, check-in and check-out?

Breakfast is a buffet, served every day from 7:30 to 9:30, and it's included in the price. Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out is by 10:00. On request and subject to availability, late check-out until 14:00 (€25) or until 18:00 (€50).

Is there parking? Do you have a private garage?

Yes. Parking is free in any available space in front of the hotel or a few metres away. A private garage (height 2.40 m) is also available on request, for a fee.

Is there somewhere to store skis?

Yes, we have a ski and boot room. The Pinzolo gondola is 30 metres down the road.

Do you accept pets?

Dogs are welcome on request, depending on room availability: please contact us before booking. We don't accept cats.

How much is the tourist tax?

It's €2 per person per night, charged for a maximum of 10 nights. Children under 14 are exempt. It isn't included in the price and is payable at the hotel, in cash.

Is the Wi-Fi free?

Yes, in every room.

How do I get the Trentino Guest Card?

If you'd like one, just ask us and we'll send it to you before you arrive.

How do I pay? Can I cancel?

Payment and cancellation terms depend on the rate you choose. You'll see them when you book. If you're unsure about anything, send us a message.
