Cookies

On this page we explain which cookies and similar tools the website uses, which ones it needs to work and which one we only use if you accept, as required by Article 122 of the Italian Privacy Code (Legislative Decree 196/2003), the GDPR and the Italian Data Protection Authority's guidelines of 10 June 2021.

Who the controller is

L&M snc di Scaramuzzi Luca e Pederzolli Manuela, which runs Hotel Garnì Maffei. Via N. Bolognini 72, 38086 Pinzolo (TN), Italy, VAT No. 02588730222. Email: info@hotelmaffeipinzolo.it. Certified email (PEC): lmsnc2020@pec.cgn.it.

For everything else about how we handle data, see our privacy policy.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website saves on your device and reads again on later visits. The same rules apply to similar tools, such as your browser's local storage.

Technical cookies: no consent needed

These tools are needed to make the website work, to remember your cookie choice and to keep payments secure. The law doesn't require consent for these (Article 122 of the Privacy Code).

Stripe's cookies come with the Beddy booking bar (Zucchetti Hospitality): the bar's code loads Stripe's, the payment provider Beddy uses, and Stripe saves its two anti-fraud cookies. No card is entered in the bar. The bar loads at your first action on the page (a tap, a click, the mouse, the keyboard or scrolling) or when a page opens already scrolled. At that moment it downloads its files and its font from Beddy's and Stripe's servers, which receive your device's IP address.

Meta pixel: only if you accept

If you click “Accept” on the banner, the website loads the Meta pixel (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd). We use it to find out how many visits come from our ads on Facebook and Instagram and how many people then open the booking page, and to show our ads to people who have already visited the website.

If you click “Reject”, or don't choose, the pixel doesn't load and nothing is sent from our website to Meta.

ToolWhoseWhyDuration
Cookie _fbpMeta, on our domainTo recognise the browser between visits, to measure our ads3 months
Cookie _fbcMeta, on our domainTo remember the click on the ad you came from3 months
Session storage “maffei-utm”OursTo remember which ad you came from, so it can be passed on to the booking engineUntil you close the browser tab

The pixel records the pages you visit, clicks on the booking buttons, your IP address and technical browser data. For collecting and transmitting this data we are joint controllers with Meta (Art. 26 GDPR, under Meta's terms for business tools); what Meta does afterwards is decided by Meta, as an independent controller, under its own policy: www.facebook.com/privacy/policy. Meta may also use its own cookies on the facebook.com domain. Data may be transferred to the United States: see our privacy policy.

You can change your mind at any time with “Cookie settings”, at the bottom of every page: if you withdraw your consent, the pixel stops and we delete the _fbp and _fbc cookies from our domain.

No analytics and no other advertising tools

We don't use any analytics tools, such as Google Analytics, or any advertising tools other than the Meta pixel.

The fonts on our pages are hosted on our own website: we don't use Google Fonts. Google Maps, Instagram, WhatsApp and the restaurants' websites only open if you tap the link, and from then on those websites' rules apply.

How you choose

On your first visit, the banner asks you whether to accept or reject the Meta pixel, with two identical buttons. If you don't choose, only the essential cookies are used and you can carry on browsing.

Your choice is kept on your device, with the date and the version of this page, and we won't ask again for 6 months, unless the list of tools changes. You can change it at any time from “Cookie settings”, at the bottom of every page.

Who hosts the website

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States). For security, GitHub records the IP address of visitors. For data transfers to the United States, see our privacy policy.

Managing cookies in your browser

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Without technical cookies the booking bar may not work: you can always book by writing to us.

Updates

This page changes if the website's tools change; if the list changes significantly, the banner will ask for your choice again. The English and Polish versions are translations: in case of doubt, the Italian text prevails.
