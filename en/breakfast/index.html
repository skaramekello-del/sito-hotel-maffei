Breakfast

What's on the buffet

We serve fresh produce, much of it from the local area.

  • SweetHomemade cakes, tarts, strudel, brioche and croissants, biscuits, jams, yoghurt, cereals, fresh fruit.
  • SavouryFresh bread (including wholemeal), cheeses, ham and cured meats, eggs on request.
  • DrinksEspresso, cappuccino, juices.

Our homemade cakes

We set them out on the buffet along with tarts and strudel.

A slice of cake, a cappuccino and off you go: the gondola is 30 metres from the hotel.

Where to eat in Pinzolo

At the Maffei, the day starts with breakfast; for dinner, Pinzolo has plenty of restaurants and pizzerias. Here are the ones we recommend to our guests, and how far each one is from the hotel.

In the village, on foot

  • Al Rocolrestaurant · on our street · 4 min walk
  • La Parzifrestaurant · 4 min walk
  • Rendenèr Alpine Foodrestaurant · 6 min walk
  • Bagatosteria and pizzeria · 8 min walk
  • Al Cardorestaurant and pizzeria · 9 min walk

Just outside Pinzolo

  • All'Antica Segheriarestaurant and pizzeria · Carisolo · 12 min walk
  • La Botterestaurant and pizzeria · Giustino · 16 min walk
  • Mildasrestaurant · Giustino · booking required · 20 min walk
  • Baita Magnabòrestaurant · towards Carisolo · 26 min walk

For a special evening

  • Grualgourmet restaurant, one Michelin star · children aged 12 and over · 10 min walk

In summer, in Val Genova

  • Chalet Val GenovaTrentino osteria · Carisolo · 2.4 km
  • Cascate Nardisrestaurant · 5.1 km
  • Chalet da Ginorestaurant · 5.5 km
  • Fontana Bonamountain refuge · 7.8 km

Between seasons some places close for a few weeks, and at the busiest times it's best to book. If in doubt, just ask us.
