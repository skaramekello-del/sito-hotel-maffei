At a glance, before you book

  • Bed & breakfast, no dinner

    We're a garni hotel: room and breakfast only, with no restaurant or half board. For dinner, head to the restaurants in the town centre.

  • 30 metres from the gondola

    The bottom station of the Pinzolo–Prà Rodont gondola is on our street. The town centre is a 5-minute walk away.

  • Winter stays

    In high season, Saturday to Saturday (7 nights) or Saturday to Friday (6 nights). In quieter periods, shorter stays are possible too.

  • Breakfast 7:30–9:30

    Included in the price, served as a buffet. Homemade cakes every day, fresh bread, cheeses and cured meats, espresso and cappuccino.

  • Ski storage and parking

    Storage for skis and boots. Free, unreserved parking in front of the hotel or a few metres away; a garage on request, at extra cost.

  • 13 rooms with private bathrooms

    All non-smoking, with shower, TV, free Wi-Fi and hairdryer. Many have a balcony, but not all.

Winter and summer

On the slopes in winter, in the Nature Park in summer

In winter you can ski at Ski Area Pinzolo, and the Pinzolo–Campiglio Express lift takes you to the Madonna di Campiglio slopes in about 16 minutes. One ski pass covers more than 150 km of pistes: the Skiarea Campiglio Dolomiti di Brenta.

In summer it's walking and cycling, with the Brenta Dolomites and the Adamello Brenta Nature Park all around. Back at the hotel, the garden and terrace are waiting for you.
