At a glance, before you book

  • Breakfast here, dinner in the village

    We're a garni hotel, offering room and breakfast. For dinner, the restaurants in the town centre are a few minutes' walk away. Restaurants we recommend

  • 30 metres from the gondola

    The Pinzolo–Prà Rodont–Doss del Sabion gondola is on our street. The town centre is a 5-minute walk away.

  • Winter stays

    In high season we prefer week-long stays, usually arriving on Saturday. At quieter times, fewer nights are possible too.

  • Summer in Val Rendena

    Hiking and cycling among the Brenta Dolomites and the Adamello Brenta Nature Park. If you'd like the Trentino Guest Card, just ask and we'll send it to you before you arrive.

  • Breakfast 7:30–9:30

    Included in the price, served as a buffet.

  • Ski storage and parking

    Storage for skis and boots. Free, unreserved parking in front of the hotel or a few metres away; a garage on request, at extra cost.

  • Garden, terrace and parking

    When you get back, our garden and terrace are waiting for you. Free, unreserved parking in front of the hotel or a few metres away; a garage on request, at extra cost.

  • 13 rooms with private bathrooms

    All non-smoking, with shower, TV, free Wi-Fi and hairdryer. Many have a balcony, but not all.

Winter and summerSummer and winter

On the slopes in winter, in the Nature Park in summerIn the Nature Park in summer, on the slopes in winter

The Hotel Garnì Maffei is in Pinzolo, in Val Rendena, a few steps from the centre of the village.

In winter you ski at Ski Area Pinzolo: the gondola is 30 metres from the hotel. The Pinzolo–Campiglio Express lift takes you to the Madonna di Campiglio pistes in about 16 minutes. One ski pass gives you more than 150 km of pistes in the Skiarea Campiglio Dolomiti di Brenta: the largest ski area in Trentino, with pistes and snowparks for every level.

In summer it's walking and cycling through woods, meadows and trails, with the Brenta Dolomites and the Adamello Brenta Nature Park all around.
